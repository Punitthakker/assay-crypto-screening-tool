# PRODUCT.md - Assay

Assay is a pre-receipt screening product for USDT and USDC. It answers one question before a transfer is accepted: is this sender, this asset, on this chain, clean enough to take right now, and how sure are we.

Audience: compliance teams at crypto exchanges, OTC desks and PSPs in UAE, KSA, India and wider MENA/APAC (buyers), plus everyday senders and receivers who want a one-line answer from a bot (top of funnel).

Positioning: the cheap, explainable pre-credit gate that runs on open data and issuer contracts, and plugs a paid analytics vendor in as one more adapter. Not a Chainalysis replacement, and says so.

Name: Assay - the metallurgist's test of a metal's purity before it is accepted. Tagline: "Assay the asset before you take it."

Brand owner: Punit Thakker. Footer credit: "Powered by MoneyBee". Neutral brand on the product itself.

Voice: short, declarative, operational. "Frozen by Tether on Tron since 12 Mar 2025." Never "may have potential risk indicators". No em dashes anywhere; hyphens only.

Surfaces: landing page (Persuade), report console (Operate), API docs (Read), Telegram bot (text).

Hard rules: no claims without data; sample data is labelled as sample; disclaimer on every surface.
