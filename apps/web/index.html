Test the asset before you take it.

Assay screens a USDT or USDC transfer before it is credited: issuer freeze, sanctions, where the funds came from, and whether the token is even real. One verdict, every reason shown, hashed for your audit file.

Six reads, one verdict

Every point of risk carries its source, the on-chain reference and the time of the check. Hard blocks override arithmetic. Confidence is reported separately, so a partial check never looks like a full one.

Score 0 to 100. CLEAR, LOW, CAUTION, HIGH, BLOCK. Scoring version pinned in every report.

Issuer freeze

Reads Tether's and Circle's own blacklist on the exact contract for the chain the funds move on. Frozen means BLOCK, whatever else is true.

isBlackListed · isBlacklisted · SPL frozen state · Tron constant call

Sanctions, two sources

OFAC's digital-currency list, refreshed nightly, and the Chainalysis on-chain oracle. A disagreement is flagged, never silently resolved.

OFAC SDN · Chainalysis oracle · UN and UAE lists via the identity layer

Source of funds

Traces inbound transfers two hops back, value-weighted and hop-decayed, against mixers, scams, frozen and sanctioned counterparties. Licensed exchange wallets end the trace, and the report says so.

Etherscan V2 · TronGrid · Helius · open label sets

Behaviour

Wallet age, pass-through speed, structuring just under thresholds, counterparty concentration, approvals to known drainers.

explorer history · threshold table per jurisdiction

Asset authenticity

Is this the issuer's contract or a look-alike? Native, bridged or Binance-pegged? Fake USDT is the most common retail loss and it is a hard block here.

registry of issuer contracts per chain · bridged variants named

Identity

Shown as unverified, attested or verified, never implied. Travel Rule data over AED 3,500 missing? The verdict floors at CAUTION and the report says why.

IVMS101 originator · Watchman name screening · GLEIF for corporates

Wallets, treasuries, protocols

Built for the UAE rulebook, useful everywhere

  • Travel Rule, AED 3,500. Fully implemented by VARA in Feb 2026. Assay floors the verdict when originator data is missing and records the gap.
  • Sanctions screening against OFAC, UN and the UAE local list is a licensing expectation, with evidence on file.
  • Enhanced due diligence on self-hosted wallets. The identity layer makes unverified senders explicit instead of invisible.
  • Audit trail. Every report is hashed and timestamped, so a decision can be reproduced for an inspector.

Frozen stablecoin inside an omnibus wallet is a direct loss and a conversation with the regulator. Assay is the gate in front of the credit.

Why exchanges run it pre-credit, not post-incident.
