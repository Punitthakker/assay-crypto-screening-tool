Reads

Six reads.
One verdict.

Every point of risk carries its source, the on-chain reference and the time of the check. Hard blocks override arithmetic. Confidence is reported separately, so a partial check never looks like a full one.

Score 000 to 100. CLEAR LOW CAUTION HIGH BLOCK. Scoring version pinned in every report.

Rulebook

Built for VARA.
Useful everywhere.

Travel RuleAED 3,500. Fully implemented Feb 2026. Assay floors the verdict when originator data is missing and records the gap.
SanctionsOFAC, UN and the UAE local list. A licensing expectation, with evidence on file.
Self-hosted walletsEnhanced due diligence. The identity layer makes unverified senders explicit instead of invisible.
Audit trailEvery report hashed and timestamped. A decision can be reproduced for an inspector.
Frozen stablecoin inside an omnibus wallet is a direct loss and a conversation with the regulator.
Assay is the gate in front of the credit.
Assay is a screening support tool. It is not legal advice and not a substitute for a regulated entity's own AML/CFT programme or a licensed blockchain analytics provider. Results reflect public data at the time of each check. Sample output on this page comes from recorded data.
