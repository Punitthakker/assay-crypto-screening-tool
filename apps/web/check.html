Check a sender before you accept.

Paste the sender's address, a transaction hash, or the tronscan / etherscan link you are looking at. Assay screens the sender against the issuer freeze list, OFAC and the Chainalysis oracle, then lists where the sender's own funds came from in the last 90 days. Nothing is stored except a hashed report id.

Tron and Solana are inferred from the address format. Explorer links set the chain.

Assay is a screening support tool. Not legal advice and not a substitute for a regulated entity's own AML/CFT programme. CLEAR means not frozen and not sanctioned at the time of the check. Sources are traced two hops back and scored as exposure (value-weighted, hop-decayed); licensed exchange wallets end the trace.
